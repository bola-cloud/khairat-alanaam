كيف يتم شحن اللحوم الطازجة؟
نقوم بتغليف اللحوم الطازجة في صناديق مبردة مخصصة مع ثلج جاف لضمان وصولها إليك بأعلى درجات الجودة والبرودة.
What is the shelf life of the meat upon delivery?
Our fresh meat has a shelf life of 3 to 5 days in the refrigerator, and up to 6 months if frozen immediately upon delivery.
Is all the meat Halal?
Yes, we guarantee that all offered meats are slaughtered according to Islamic Sharia and are documented with official Halal certificates.
Where is the Wagyu meat imported from?
We import premium Wagyu meat from top certified farms in Australia and Japan to ensure superior quality and perfect marbling.
